Support

Drillbound · Last updated: August 7, 2026

Support email:

purecodebase@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, the planet or screen where the issue occurred, and a short description. Screenshots are helpful. Never send passwords, payment-card details, certificates, or API keys.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

  • World 1 is playable without an account or purchase.
  • The Full Game is one optional, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase.
  • If a verified purchase is not visible after reinstalling, open the purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while using the same Apple Account.
  • Apple handles payment and refund decisions. Visit Apple's refund guidance for a refund request.

Save data and offline play

Runs, upgrades, planet progress, and settings are stored locally on the device. There is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove local progress permanently. Purchase verification and Restore Purchases may require an internet connection.

Controls and layout

For touch-control, HUD, safe-area, or screen-layout issues, include your iPhone model, iOS version, screen orientation, and a screenshot if possible. Tell us whether the issue happens during a fresh run, in the depot, or on the purchase screen.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Read the Privacy Policy for details about local saves, support messages, and Apple purchase status.
