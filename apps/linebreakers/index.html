LineBreakers

A lane-based hero defence game for iPhone and iPad. Three heroes fire on their own; you drag them between five lanes, time their skills and break the line with a team ultimate while goblins, ogres and chapter bosses push toward your barricade.

  • Three heroes, five lanes
  • iPhone and iPad
  • Landscape mode
  • No account required
  • Offline core
  • Free with ads · optional purchases

Terms of Service

Terms for the offline core, advertising, and the optional in-app purchases.

Read terms
LineBreakers has no account system, developer-operated backend, or first-party analytics. Missions and local progress work offline. The free iOS version uses Google Mobile Ads with UMP consent for occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos, can request ATT where applicable, and offers in-app purchases through the App Store. Ads, consent, and purchase or restore flows may require a network connection. The game is in English.
