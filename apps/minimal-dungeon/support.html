Progress and Saves

Game progress is stored locally. There is no account or cloud save. If the app is deleted or its data is reset, progress may not be recoverable.

Ads and Privacy

Ad availability depends on network access, consent, region, frequency limits, and inventory. Closing a rewarded ad before earning its reward grants no revive. Privacy options are available from Settings when required. Report inappropriate ads with the approximate time and a screenshot if possible.
