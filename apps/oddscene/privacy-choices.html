Privacy Choices

For: OddScene: Spot the Difference · Last updated: September 20, 2026

Current status: The iOS release can use Google Mobile Ads and UMP consent for optional rewarded hints and interstitials. The app has no first-party analytics. UMP and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choices can affect advertising and tracking availability.

1. Local game data

Progress, scores, stars, tutorial state, selected scene, and accessibility or audio preferences are stored locally on the device. The current MVP does not upload this data to PureCodeBase. Deleting the app or clearing its local data may remove it permanently.

2. Tracking and advertising

  • UMP updates consent information before the app requests rewarded or interstitial ads. If UMP does not allow ad requests, the app does not load or show those ads.
  • Depending on region and consent, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. Consent choices can be changed through the applicable UMP privacy form.
  • The app can request ATT before advertising. Denying ATT prevents the related tracking authorization; it does not automatically disable every ad request allowed by UMP.
  • Settings includes a PRIVACY OPTIONS entry when the advertising provider is available. The entry opens the provider's privacy choices form.

3. Purchases and local progress

The optional Full Version is a one-time non-consumable purchase handled by Apple StoreKit 2. Apple controls payment and purchase processing. Restore uses Apple's transaction synchronization, while the verified entitlement is cached locally so the game can apply the purchase without an account.

Progress, scores, stars, tutorial state, selected scene, and accessibility or audio preferences stay in the local app container. They are not uploaded to a PureCodeBase server.

4. Requests

For a privacy question or rights request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Provider details are available in the Impressum.
