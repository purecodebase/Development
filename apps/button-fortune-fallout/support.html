Purchases and Privacy

  • The free slice is playable without a purchase and the game contains no advertising.
  • The optional full-game unlock is a single non-consumable purchase handled by Apple through the App Store.
  • If a purchase is not visible after reinstalling, use the restore option in the purchase screen.
  • The app does not request tracking permission and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • A public summary of these controls is available here: Privacy Choices.

Gameplay and Save Data

  • Run progress, best day, unlocked endings, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • If the app is removed or app data is cleared, progress may be lost and may not be recoverable.
  • Purchase availability can depend on App Store account state, storefront, device settings, and temporary App Store connectivity.
