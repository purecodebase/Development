purecodebase@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, screen orientation, a short description, and a screenshot or recording when useful. Never send passwords, payment-card details, certificates, or API keys.

Gameplay and layout

  • For a touch, safe-area, screen-reader, frame-rate, or layout issue, tell us the device model and the screen where it occurred.
  • OddScene is designed for fixed portrait layouts on iPhone and iPad. The current MVP does not use scrolling, panning, zooming, or swipe navigation.
  • If a scene or change appears visually incorrect, include the level name or number and a screenshot if possible.

Progress and offline play

Game progress, stars, scores, tutorial state, and settings are stored locally on the device. There is no account or cloud save in the current MVP. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove local progress and it may not be recoverable.

Purchases and advertising

The optional Full Version is a one-time purchase processed through Apple StoreKit 2. Use RESTORE PURCHASES if a verified purchase is not shown on the device. Prices and availability come from the App Store. The free iOS path can request rewarded ads for hints and show an interstitial after a completed level; consent and advertising may require network access. If ads are unavailable because of privacy choices or connectivity, the game continues without the ad.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or rights request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Read the Privacy Policy and Privacy Choices for details about local saves, Google advertising services, Apple purchases, and consent options.
