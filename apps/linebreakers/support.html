Heroes and lanes

  • Your Gunner, Heavy and Tech fire on their own. Drag a hero to another lane, or select a hero and tap a lane; moving into an occupied lane swaps the two heroes.
  • Each hero has a skill tree; equip two skills per hero and rank them up in HEROES. Fill the team gauge to trigger the Line Breaker.
  • Enemies that warn before they spawn, such as the ogre, show their lane first: move a hero there in time.
  • A mission is lost when the barricade falls or the whole squad is down. TRY AGAIN restarts it at no cost.

Modes

Each chapter opens a Hard tab once its finale is won, and a NIGHTMARE tab once the finale is won on Hard. ENDLESS on the map starts a never-ending run in the chapter's arena and keeps your best wave. DAILY in the hub offers one mission per day; it uses your device's date and stays closed while the device clock reads earlier than the last time the game saw.

Progress and offline play

Heroes, gear, and progress are stored locally on the device. There is no account or cloud save. Missions play without a network connection. Deleting the app removes local progress, and it may not be recoverable.

Purchases and advertising

Gold packs, the forge crate, the starter pack, and the Ad-Free Pass are sold in the in-game SHOP (hub). Prices come from the App Store. If a one-time purchase is missing on a device, use RESTORE in the SHOP with the same Apple Account.

The free version shows no interstitial ads in the first three missions. After that, a full-screen ad may appear when you leave a result screen, at most once every six minutes, never during a mission. Rewarded videos are always your choice: they double a victory chest, double the daily chest, or continue an Endless run once. If ads are unavailable because of privacy choices or connectivity, the game continues without them.
