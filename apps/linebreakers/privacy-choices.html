Privacy Choices

For: LineBreakers · Last updated: October 18, 2026

Current status: The free iOS version uses Google Mobile Ads and UMP consent for occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded videos. The app has no first-party analytics. UMP and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choices affect advertising and tracking. The Ad-Free Pass removes ads entirely; with it active, the app does not start the advertising or consent SDKs.

1. Local game data

Heroes, gear, currencies, campaign and Endless progress, daily mission dates, owned one-time purchases, granted store transactions, the time of the last ad, and settings are stored locally on the device. They are not uploaded to PureCodeBase. Deleting the app removes them.

2. Tracking and advertising

  • UMP updates consent information before the app requests interstitial or rewarded ads. If UMP does not allow ad requests, the app does not load or show those ads, and the game continues without them.
  • Depending on region and consent, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.
  • The app can request ATT through Google's consent flow. Denying ATT keeps the advertising identifier (IDFA) unavailable; it does not automatically disable every ad request allowed by UMP. You can change ATT later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • The game's Settings page shows a PRIVACY button when the consent provider requires it, for example in the EEA and the UK. The button opens Google's privacy options form, where you can change or withdraw consent.

3. Purchases

Gold packs and the forge crate are consumable purchases; the starter pack and the Ad-Free Pass are one-time non-consumable purchases. All are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Ad-Free Pass removes interstitial ads and grants rewarded-video bonuses without a video. With the Ad-Free Pass active, the app does not start Google Mobile Ads or the UMP consent flow at launch; a pass bought during a session stops ads at once and the SDKs stay off from the next launch. Apple controls payment and purchase processing; RESTORE in the in-game SHOP uses Apple's transaction records, and the result is stored locally so the game works without an account.

4. Requests

For a privacy question or rights request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Provider details are available in the Impressum.
