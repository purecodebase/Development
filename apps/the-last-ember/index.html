The Last Ember

An offline-first narrative dark-fantasy roguelite for iOS. Carry a dying flame through a broken kingdom, where every seed, decision, relationship, and consequence shapes the road home.

Narrative Roguelite Dark Fantasy Offline-first No account required

One flame. Many destinies.

The beacons of Eldervale are failing. You are the unseen bearer chosen to carry the last ember to the final lighthouse. A deterministic seed changes the omen, routes, figures, companions, resources, and possible endings. The game remembers what you choose and brings those decisions back when the road turns.
