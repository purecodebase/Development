Privacy Choices

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Advertising Privacy Options

Where Google requires a privacy-options entry, open Minimal Dungeon → Settings → Advertising privacy choices. The app uses Google User Messaging Platform to present the available controls. Availability depends on region and the current consent requirement.

Tracking Permission

Minimal Dungeon does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Google Mobile Ads still processes data for advertising and related purposes as described in the Privacy Policy, subject to consent, platform rules, and device settings.

Local Game Data

Runs, progress, statistics, discoveries, achievements, Daily history, and settings are stored on your device. Settings contains reset controls for this information. Deleting the app also removes its local container, subject to normal iOS behavior and device backups.

Requests

For privacy questions or rights requests, email purecodebase@gmail.com.
