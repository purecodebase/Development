Otter Dive

An offline portrait diving adventure for iOS. Guide a curious otter from the island to the hadal frontier, collect discoveries, avoid predators, and make it back safely.

Diving Adventure Offline-first No Ads One-time FullAccess

Dive deep. Return with the story.

Explore five thousand metres of ocean, build ten-level upgrades, fill your collection, and choose when to turn back. The Free Expedition includes progression through Level 5 and a 2,050 m depth cap. One optional, permanent FullAccess purchase unlocks Levels 6–10, the 5,000 m frontier, Hadal Gleam, and the Megalodon endgame. Otter Dive has no subscriptions, advertising, or premium currency.
