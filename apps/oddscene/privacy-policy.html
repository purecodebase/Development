Privacy Policy

For: OddScene: Spot the Difference

Effective and last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Controller and scope

This policy applies only to OddScene: Spot the Difference, an iPhone and iPad game published by PureCodeBase and operated by Nico Würthenberger, Bissingerstraße 8, 75172 Pforzheim, Germany.

Contact: purecodebase@gmail.com. Provider details are available in the Impressum.

2. Local game data

OddScene stores the information needed to continue the game in the app's local container. This can include level completion, stars, scores, streaks, attempts, the last selected scene, first-run tutorial state, and local preferences such as Reduced Motion, haptics, and sound effects.

This data is not uploaded to a PureCodeBase server. OddScene has no account system, login, cloud save, or first-party online profile. Deleting the app or clearing its local data may permanently remove local progress.

3. Current MVP services and permissions

  • The current MVP is designed for offline play and does not require a network connection or an account.
  • The current MVP does not include first-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or an active third-party advertising SDK.
  • The current MVP does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use cross-app tracking.
  • The current MVP does not require contacts, location, camera, microphone, or notification access.
  • Development hooks for a possible future StoreKit purchase or advertising integration are not live data-processing services in the current build.

4. Support messages

If you email support or a privacy request, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, a description, or screenshots. We use that information to answer the request, investigate the reported issue, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use support messages for advertising or profiling.

5. Apple and future services

Apple distributes the app through its platform and processes platform data under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. The current MVP does not complete live in-app purchases. If a later release enables a Full Version purchase, advertising, consent management, or another third-party service, this policy will be revised to identify the active provider, data categories, and available choices before that service is enabled for that release.

6. Retention and sharing

Local game data remains on your device until you reset it, delete the app, or the platform removes it. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or share local game progress with third parties.

7. Children

OddScene is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the current MVP does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics, it does not intentionally request personal information from players.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal information, and to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. Contact purecodebase@gmail.com for a request. You may also contact the relevant supervisory authority.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, its data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated with material revisions.
