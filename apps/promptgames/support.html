Prompts and external chats

PromptGames creates the game prompt locally. Use Copy, the iOS Share Sheet, or Copy & Open to move it to a chat service yourself. The app does not send prompts automatically and does not require an external chat account for its local catalog and configuration screens.

Saved games and Game Night

Favorites, history, saved setups, groups, house rules, playlists, collection records, and challenges are stored locally. There is no account or cloud save. Deleting the app or clearing its local data may remove this information permanently.

Full Unlock and Restore Purchases

  • Six templates are free; one optional non-consumable purchase unlocks the other 44.
  • If you were charged but the catalog is still locked, restart the app and use Restore Purchases from the paywall or Settings.
  • Apple handles payment processing and refund decisions. Use Apple’s refund guidance for a refund request.

Accessibility and language

PromptGames includes German and English game content and settings for appearance, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, Reduce Motion, and haptics. Tell us your language, accessibility setting, device size, and the screen that was difficult to use.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Read the Privacy Policy for details about local data, Apple purchases, sharing, and external chat websites.
