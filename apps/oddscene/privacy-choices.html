Privacy Choices

For: OddScene: Spot the Difference · Last updated: September 20, 2026

Current status: The current OddScene MVP does not use advertising, cross-app tracking, an analytics SDK, or an active consent provider. There is therefore no advertising-consent prompt or ATT permission flow to manage in the current build.

1. Local game data

Progress, scores, stars, tutorial state, selected scene, and accessibility or audio preferences are stored locally on the device. The current MVP does not upload this data to PureCodeBase. Deleting the app or clearing its local data may remove it permanently.

2. Tracking and advertising

  • OddScene does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in the current MVP.
  • OddScene does not use cross-app tracking or personalized advertising in the current MVP.
  • No ad or consent SDK is active in the current MVP, so there are no in-app advertising choices to reopen.

3. Future optional services

The project contains a platform-neutral boundary for a possible Full Version purchase and optional advertising in a later release. Those integrations are not active in the current MVP. If they are enabled, the in-app Settings flow and this page will be updated with the applicable consent, tracking, purchase, restore, and provider information before release.

4. Requests

For a privacy question or rights request, email purecodebase@gmail.com. Provider details are available in the Impressum.
