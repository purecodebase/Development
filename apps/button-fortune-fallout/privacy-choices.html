For: Button: Fortune & Fallout

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. No Advertising Consent

  • Button: Fortune & Fallout does not display advertising.
  • The app does not show an advertising consent message because it does not request advertising data.
  • No advertising identifier or advertising measurement is used.

2. No Tracking Permission

  • The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission.
  • The app does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Local Save Data

  • Run progress, best day, unlocked endings, settings, and similar gameplay data are stored locally on your device.
  • If you remove the app or clear its local data, this gameplay data may be lost and may not be recoverable.

4. Optional Premium Purchase

  • The free slice is playable without a purchase.
  • One optional, non-consumable in-app purchase permanently unlocks the full game.
  • Apple handles payment details and purchase records through the App Store.
